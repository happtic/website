Jihanki Town privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

In short: Jihanki Town doesn't collect any personal information itself. Your game is saved on your device, and in your own iCloud if you use Game Center, and never sent to us. There are no accounts and no analytics. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Those ads are not personalised, and the game never asks to track you, but Google does collect some information to show them, as described below.

Who we are

Jihanki Town is made by Hamish Rickerby at happtic, an independent software studio. In this policy, "we" means happtic. You can reach us at contact@happtic.com.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your saved game and settings (sound and haptics) are stored on your device. We never receive them. "Start over" in the game's Settings resets your game, and deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.
  • No accounts. You don't sign up for anything, and we don't ask for your name, email address or age.
  • No analytics. The game doesn't use any analytics or tracking service.

Apple's services

  • Game Center. If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends the badges you earn to Apple, so you can see them there. It also keeps a copy of your town in your iCloud, so you can carry on playing on your other iPhones and iPads. That copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account, and we can't see it. Apple handles all of this under Apple's privacy policy. We don't see your Game Center details.
  • Remove ads. The optional Remove ads purchase is handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your payment details, only whether the purchase was made.

Ads, from Google AdMob

The game shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen once you've played for a while, and offers optional bonus ads that only play when you tap to watch one. These ads come from Google AdMob. We've set them up to be as gentle as we can:

  • Not personalised. Every ad request asks Google for ads that aren't based on your interests or what you do in other apps.
  • No tracking. The game never asks for permission to track you, so neither the game nor Google gets your device's advertising identifier.
  • Family-friendly. Ads are limited to Google's G rating, suitable for all ages, and we block sensitive categories such as gambling, dating and alcohol.

To show ads, measure them and keep them working, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • your approximate location, worked out from your internet (IP) address;
  • an identifier for the app on your device, which isn't the advertising identifier;
  • which ads were shown and whether they were tapped or watched;
  • crash reports and performance information.

We don't receive this information. Google handles it under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google privacy policy.

If you buy Remove ads, the banner stops. The bonus ads are still offered, but only play if you choose to watch one.

Europe and the UK

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices the first time an ad is needed, and no ads load before you answer. You can change your choices at any time from Settings → Ad choices in the game.

Children

Jihanki Town is a game for everyone, and families play it together. Because the game collects no personal information itself, we don't collect any from children. The ads are limited to the G rating and are never personalised.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there's nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. For information Google holds, use your Google choices above, or Google's own privacy tools. If you have any questions, email contact@happtic.com.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the App Store.
